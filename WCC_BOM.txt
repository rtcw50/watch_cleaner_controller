Note: the metal tubing in this BOM is only 30mm in diameter.
All the Fusion360 drawings used a 32mm diameter central tower tube.
Please adjust the Fusion drawings which interface with the tower to
have a 30mm diameter or find a substitute 400mm tower with a 32mm diameter.

Note: There are a lot of optimizations that could be made in this
project build. Using an existing 12v power supply instead of 24v for
example. The highest cost items are the jars and the cleaning baskets.
Cheaper jars could be substituted for sure. The cleaning baskets are
difficult to source and/or build. So you may be stuck buying these.
A homemade DC-DC converter could be used (using a 7805 chip) to reduce
costs further. The project box could be printed. 

Many of these parts could be sourced from AliExpress for better prices.
